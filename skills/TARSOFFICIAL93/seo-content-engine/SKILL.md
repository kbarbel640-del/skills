# SEO Content Engine

Generate complete SEO-optimized blog posts in minutes. This skill performs keyword research, analyzes top-ranking content, and produces structured articles ready for publication.

## 🎯 What It Does

1. **Keyword Research** — Validates search volume and competition for your target keyword
2. **Competitor Analysis** — Fetches and analyzes top 5 ranking articles
3. **Content Generation** — Creates structured article with headings, meta description, and internal linking suggestions
4. **SEO Optimization** — Includes Schema.org markup suggestions, readability scoring, and keyword density analysis

## ⚙️ Technical Implementation

### Required Tools
- `web_search` — Keyword research and competitor discovery
- `web_fetch` — Competitor content analysis
- `read/write` — Template and output management

### Workflow

1. **Input Processing**
   - Parse keyword and parameters
   - Validate input quality

2. **Research Phase**
   - Search for target keyword
   - Identify top 5 ranking URLs
   - Fetch and analyze competitor content

3. **Analysis Phase**
   - Extract common headings/structure
   - Identify content gaps
   - Analyze word count and depth

4. **Generation Phase**
   - Create outline based on analysis
   - Generate sections with proper keyword placement
   - Optimize for readability and SEO

5. **Output Phase**
   - Format complete article
   - Generate metadata and checklist
   - Provide competitor insights
