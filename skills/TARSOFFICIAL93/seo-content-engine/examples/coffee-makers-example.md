## Setting Up the Perfect Home Office Coffee Station

### Location Tips
1. Place near a water source for easy refilling
2. Keep 3 feet from electronics to prevent steam damage
3. Position away from direct sunlight to preserve bean quality
4. Ensure easy access without leaving your desk area

### Essential Accessories
- Insulated travel mug for moving between rooms
- Coffee bean storage container (airtight)
- Small trash bin for pods/grounds
- Microfiber cloth for daily cleaning

## Maintenance Tips for Longevity

### Daily
- Empty drip tray and used pods/grounds
- Wipe exterior with damp cloth
- Run water through if not used for 8+ hours

### Weekly
- Deep clean removable parts
- Descale if you have hard water
- Check water filter (if applicable)

### Monthly
- Full descaling cycle
- Replace water filter
- Inspect seals and gaskets

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Buying based on price alone** — Cheap machines cost more in frustration and replacement
❌ **Ignoring noise levels** — A loud brewer disrupts video calls
❌ **Forgetting about cleanup** — Complex machines often go unused
❌ **Wrong size for your needs** — Single person doesn't need a 12-cup carafe

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the best coffee maker for a small home office?
The Nespresso VertuoPlus or Keurig Mini are ideal for limited space while delivering quality coffee.

### How much should I spend on a home office coffee maker?
For most professionals, $150-250 hits the sweet spot of quality, features, and durability.

### Are single-serve coffee makers worth it?
Yes, if you value convenience and variety. The cost per cup is higher ($0.50-1.20) but still far cheaper than cafés.

### Can I write off a coffee maker as a business expense?
Potentially yes, if used exclusively for your home office. Consult your tax professional for specifics.

### How do I reduce coffee maker noise during calls?
Look for machines with "quiet brew" technology, or schedule brewing before/after meetings.
